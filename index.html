About us

A virtual workshop for the DACH region

Founded in 2022, Remote Factory bridges the gap between the DACH region's demand for engineering talent and Slovakia's pool of highly qualified specialists.

Every team member holds a Master's or PhD degree in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, computer science, 3D art, or supply chain management. Our team communicates fluently in English and German — ensuring seamless integration with your workflow.
