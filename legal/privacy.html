When you visit our website, our web server automatically records the following data:

This data is processed on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 31 nDSG / Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) to ensure the security and proper functioning of the website.

3.2 Contact via Email / Phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your name, email address, phone number, and the content of your message for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. The legal basis is legitimate interests or, where applicable, the performance of a contract (Art. 31 nDSG / Art. 6(1)(b)(f) GDPR). Data is retained for 3 years unless a longer retention period is legally required.
