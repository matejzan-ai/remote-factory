Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 2024

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences and settings between visits.

Cookies we use

This website uses only essential cookies — the minimum required for basic functionality. We do not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies of any kind.

Essential cookies (localStorage)

We use browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store two preference values:

  • rf_cookie_consent — Stores your cookie consent decision (accepted / declined). Persists until you clear browser data.
  • rf_lang — Stores your language preference (EN or DE). Persists until you clear browser data.

No third-party cookies

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, or any other third-party tracking technology on this website.

External fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). This request may log your IP address per Google's privacy policy. You can disable this by using a browser extension that blocks Google Fonts.

Managing cookies

You can clear stored preferences at any time through your browser's developer tools or by clearing site data. Most browsers also allow you to block all cookies in their privacy settings.
