Partners

We collaborate with carefully selected partners to deliver comprehensive, integrated solutions from a single source.

Riwers is a software house that supports you in all aspects of software development. From the analysis of your initial situation, your new ideas and their concretization up to the implementation of applications, apps or entire systems. Depending on where you stand, you will receive the following customized services from Riwers.

  • Solution projects – Riwers implements turnkey development projects and delivers your customized solution.
  • Extended Workbench – Riwers provides development power for your project or development organization (whole teams, individual experts, specific skills).
  • Scoping & Requirements Management – Riwers elaborates your idea to make it actionable.
  • Collaboration Setup – Together with you, Riwers builds your perfect organization so that it acts as a software delivery machine.
  • Digital Journey Compass – Riwers creates with you your clear and concrete path to digitalize your business.

Our partnership with Riwers merges the hardware and software worlds for a holistic and integrated digitalisation of products and projects from a single source.
