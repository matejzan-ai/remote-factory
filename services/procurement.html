Three pillars of our procurement approach

1. Procurement Market Analysis

Based on your questions and requirements, we provide you with a clear overview of the possibilities and limits of the industrial procurement market — with a focus on Central and Eastern Europe.

2. Supplier Evaluation

We separate the wheat from the chaff for you. Using measurable criteria for systematic and transparent evaluation — including on-site visits, technical assessments, and quality audits.

3. Supplier Support & Ramp-Up

We make sure you succeed. Sampling, process implementation, quality standards, auditing, and continuous improvement are managed and monitored on site — by our specialists who are local to your suppliers.

Full scope of services

  • Building supply chains for new products
  • Interface between R&D, purchasing, industrialization, and ramp-up management
  • Cost reduction / Cost Redesign
  • Project-related operational purchasing
  • Nearshoring and local sourcing in Slovakia, Czech Republic, Hungary
  • Global procurement and electronic components sourcing
  • Category management: establishment and execution
  • On-site representation of client interests
