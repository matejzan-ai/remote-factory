Digital Twin Application

The Digital Twin Application extends the benefits of your products. Use digital manuals, support technicians with animated maintenance instructions, or optimise your spare parts business — all through an interactive 3D web experience.

Engineering Visualisation Digital Twin Digitalisation Procurement On-Demand

More than visualisation

The Digital Twin Application is about digital integration — making complex things simple, accessible, and available at all times. It runs in any modern web browser, requires no app installation, and can be embedded directly into your existing systems.

Explore our Digital Twin Application directly below — no download or installation required.

Use cases

  • Interactive digital manuals replacing static PDFs
  • Animated maintenance instructions for field technicians
  • Digital spare parts catalogue with 3D part identification
  • AI-assisted support for customers and service partners
  • 3D model exploration for sales and product training
  • Procedure tracking and step-by-step guided workflows

Interactive Demonstrators

We have built demonstrators across product categories to show the range of what the Digital Twin Application can do:

3D Radial Engine

Complex engineering systems and precision mechanisms — demonstrating how intricate moving parts can be explored interactively, with assembly/disassembly animations and component identification.

3D RoboVac

Consumer products and everyday smart devices — showing how product features, maintenance procedures, and troubleshooting can be communicated visually to end customers.

3D Hydrant

Industrial equipment with detailed component access — demonstrating digital twin use for infrastructure and utility products, enabling remote inspection and virtual training.
