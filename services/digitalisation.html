Digitalisation Services

We do the hard work for you — bringing your existing product data up to date (2D → 3D → VR) and building the digital foundation for AI, automation, and future innovation.

Engineering Visualisation Digital Twin Digitalisation Procurement On-Demand

Three core services

1. Digital Transformation

Many companies sit on a 'digitalisation backlog' — products from the 1990s and 2000s that exist only in 2D drawings or outdated 3D formats. We systematically convert this legacy data into modern, parameterizable formats — freeing your internal team to focus on development rather than maintenance.

2. Reverse Engineering

We create digital product data and 3D models from outdated analogue data and drawings. Scan data (point clouds) are transformed into parameterizable standard CAD formats such as STEP — giving your products a complete, accurate digital representation.

3. Digital Innovation Strategy

We help you navigate the landscape of technological development opportunities and risks — identifying the strategic directions that are right for your company and products, based on practical experience rather than abstract consulting.
