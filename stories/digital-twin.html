Success Stories — Digital Twin

The Digital Twin Application offers more than visualisation — it's about digital integration, making complex things simple, accessible, and available at all times.

Engineering Visualisation Digital Twin Digitalisation Procurement

Demonstrator: 3D Radial Engine

Focus: complex engineering systems and precision mechanisms. An interactive 3D web application allowing full exploration of a radial engine — component identification, assembly/disassembly sequences, and animated operation. Runs directly in the browser with no installation required.

Demonstrator: 3D RoboVac

Focus: consumer products and everyday smart devices. Demonstrates how product features, maintenance procedures, and troubleshooting can be communicated visually to end customers — replacing traditional paper manuals and reducing support calls.

Demonstrator: 3D Hydrant

Focus: industrial equipment with detailed component access. Demonstrates digital twin use for infrastructure and utility products — enabling remote inspection, virtual training, and interactive spare parts identification for field service teams.
