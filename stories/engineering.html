Re-Design for Additive Manufacturing

Challenge

Individual small washing baskets for watch parts are required in different dimensions. For manufacturing, only 3D metal printing works — requiring design optimisation for the process.

Approach

One specialist, 10–20% effort over one month. Needs assessment, model analysis, design optimisation, and iterative refinement based on production testing.

Result

  • Production-ready designs for metal 3D printing
  • Optimised lattice structures through multiple test cycles
  • Four different basket variants for various part groups

Prototype of Mobile PV Unit

Situation

Customer had a photovoltaic product concept and technical understanding, but lacked R&D resources to bring it from idea to testable prototype.

Approach

One experienced developer, 20–40% over six months. Online onboarding, 3D concept studies, strength calculations, prototype development for field feasibility testing.

Result

  • Iterative mechanical improvements from field test feedback
  • Complete STEP datasets with initial cost estimates
  • Production-ready prototype delivered within budget

  • Continuity through long-term collaboration since 2022
