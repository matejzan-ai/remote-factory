Prototype of a Digital Product Twin

Challenge

Customer sought innovative ideas for further digitalisation of products and services — needed to make product knowledge accessible to customers, service partners, and employees, with no existing digital infrastructure.

Approach

One specialist, 50% over 3 months. Business case development, application area definition, Digital Twin structure and functionality design, interactive prototype creation.

Result

  • Proof of technical feasibility
  • Investment requirement specifications per product
  • Foundation for new business model development

Transformation 2D to 3D — Product Library

Challenge

Products from the 1990s–2000s existed only in 2D or outdated 3D formats. The company lacked internal capacity to build a modern digital product library — internal designers were needed for active development, not digitisation backlog.

Approach

Two designers, 50–100% over 12 months. Online team onboarding and project planning, systematic conversion of 2D drawings to 3D models (STEP format), design error correction and technical update implementation.

Result

  • Extensive digitisation backlog resolved efficiently
  • Internal designers freed for development focus
  • Cost-effective, flexible staffing over 12 months
