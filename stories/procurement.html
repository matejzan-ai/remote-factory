Global Sourcing of Electronic Components

Challenge

Supply bottleneck for electronic components with a dried-up market. Internal capacity insufficient to locate new sources in time to avoid production stoppage.

Approach

One specialist, 50–100% over five months. Team onboarding and product familiarisation, research and evaluation of new supply sources with quote comparisons, independent purchasing on the global electronic components market.

Result

  • Supply situation stabilised after 3–4 months
  • New supplier relationships established
  • Short-term capacity needs fulfilled without delay

GRP/CRP Supplier Evaluation

Situation

Existing ventilation wheel mockup lacked industrialisation expertise and market access for a specialised composite manufacturing partnership in Central Europe.

Approach

One specialist in composite development/production, 25–50% for six months. Market analysis across Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Hungary with on-site visits to three preferred suppliers.

Result

  • Market overview and comprehensive supplier evaluation
  • Three qualified supplier pre-selections
  • Technical clarification for prototype and series development

Supply Chain Optimisation

Challenge

Delivery failures and quality issues with an existing Central European supplier — declining confidence from the Swiss production team and escalating pressure to resolve or replace.

Approach

One specialist, 40–60% capacity over four months. Supplier audits on-site, action plan development and implementation, coordination between supplier and Swiss production.

Result

  • No more delivery failures after 2 months
  • Stabilised quality with effective controls in place
  • Consistent component availability restored
