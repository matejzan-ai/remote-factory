Success Stories — Visualisation

Engineering Visualisation Digital Twin Digitalisation Procurement

Wenger Fenster — Airflow Technology Animation

When the USP is hidden, visualisation becomes essential. This project showcased automatic air exchange within window frames for Wenger Fenster — revealing an invisible process that delivers fresh air without opening windows. The animation communicates a complex technical advantage that no photograph or brochure could.

Gilden Door Systems — SL15 Drive Unit

Remote Factory demonstrated how virtual reality enables product showcase capabilities impossible in the physical world. The SL15 from Gilden Door Systems highlights features and capabilities through immersive visualisation — streamlining communication with customers, sales, and service partners while supporting internal training.

Rotary Transfer Machine — Technical Animation

Inspiring customers by showing the strengths and USPs of complex manufacturing equipment in photorealistic quality. Hidden functions and processes are brought to light — the animation serves as both a sales tool and technical documentation.

CNC Laser Cutting Machine — PDF to Animation

Complete conversion from PDF manuals to technical animation — demonstrating machine assembly, operation, and functionality comprehensively. The result replaced a stack of static documents with an intuitive, interactive experience that technicians and operators actually use.

University Gearbox — 3D Visualisation & Spare Parts Catalogue

Two-dimensional sketches converted to 3D models with animation and a digital spare parts catalogue. Gearbox visualisation created for educational use at a university — combining precision engineering accuracy with accessible interactive presentation.
